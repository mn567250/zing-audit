How the y-scale behaves

SituationAutomatic scaleNotes
No probe yetFull rangeProbe focus is disabled until there is probe data.
Probe runningProbe focusThe y-axis fits 1.25 × the probe's p95 and is re-fitted with each new request. Requests above the range become ▲ markers on the top edge, with a count in the corner.
Probe finishedFull range or Keep focusOpen question, so it is a prototype switch above. Full range matches "probe focus while the probe runs" but brings the squash back in finished reports. Keep focus stays readable.
You pick a scaleYour choiceAutomatic switching stops and the label shows "set by you". "Back to automatic" hands control back. A new audit starts automatic again.

Accessibility

  1. The switch is a labelled group of two toggle buttons with aria-pressed. They work with Tab, Space and Enter, and their target size is at least 30 px.
  2. Automatic switches are announced in a polite live region ("Y-axis switched to probe focus"). The visible label always says whether the scale is automatic or set by you. The chart only dims and fades back in briefly, and not at all with reduced motion.
  3. Nothing is hidden: ▲ markers are part of the hover and keyboard order, and their tooltip gives the exact value. The chart's accessible name states the scale and how many requests are above it.
  4. Shape as well as colour: audit requests are outlined, probe requests are small and translucent, failures are ×, and requests above the range are ▲. The median line and band carry the probe values that a single faint dot cannot.
